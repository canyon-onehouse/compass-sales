# Compass Design Studio

Domain language for the Compass Design Studio marketing site — a residential design and interiors practice headquartered in Northern Alabama, serving clients nationwide.

## Language

**Project**:
A bespoke design engagement with a specific client. Never carries a price. Exactly four exist at launch: Hurricane Ranch, Widner Residence, Hickerson Home, Aubrey Place. A Project that results from a Plan is a bespoke customization for that client, never a reusable variant.
_Avoid_: commission, client engagement, active work, job

**Plan**:
A ready-to-build drawing set designed in advance, purchasable for construction on the buyer's own lot. Will carry a price once commerce launches. Site-facing label: "Ready-to-build plan".
_Avoid_: pre-built plan, house plan, option

**Work**:
The umbrella for everything the studio shows publicly — Projects and Plans together.
_Avoid_: portfolio, projects (as the umbrella term)

**Inquiry**:
A general lead captured by the site's main contact form, qualified on budget, timeline, and ownership.
_Avoid_: contact, submission

**Plan request**:
A lead expressing interest in a specific Plan, captured by a dedicated form distinct from the Inquiry form and attributed to the Plan it came from.
_Avoid_: plan inquiry, purchase request

**Design Essentials**:
A named service package — a 30-day process ending in schematic plans. The name appears publicly (FAQ) as a teaser; its contents and pricing stay unpublished while services are market-tested through sales calls.
